About Us – Kuchaman Defence Academy (KDA)

🌟 Why Choose KDA

KDA, Kuchaman City (Nagaur, Rajasthan) – a premier institute for Airforce, Navy, Army, ICG, NDA, SSC GD & Delhi Police. We blend classroom rigour with modern analytics, doubt labs, hostel and hybrid learning to turn aspirants into achievers.

Founder of KDA

Kuchaman Defence Academy, a premier institution dedicated to forging the next generation of India’s defence leaders. Our foundation is built upon the extensive vision and expertise of our founder, Mr. Vinod Choudhary, who brings over two decades of dedicated experience to the field of defence education. With 20 years of unparalleled guidance, Mr. Choudhary has cultivated an environment of discipline, integrity, and excellence. Our mission is to provide aspirants with comprehensive training, strategic knowledge
