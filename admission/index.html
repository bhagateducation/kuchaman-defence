Admission Process

01

Take a Tour or Contact

Visit the official admission page or contact via WhatsApp/Call. Provide your basic details: Name, Mobile Number, Course, City, and Batch Preference.

02

Free Counselling & Batch Selection

Our counsellor will guide you about suitable batches (Regular / Crash / Online).Course details, fee structure, and hostel facilities will be explained clearly.

03

Document Verification & Fee Submission

Submit essential documents: Aadhar, Marksheet, Photos, and Caste Certificate (if applicable). Pay fees via UPI, Bank Transfer, Cash, or Online Payment Gateway.

04

Batch Allotment & Orientation

Get your batch schedule, study material, and access to test series.Attend the orientation session and start your defence preparation journey.

Required enrollment documents

Please carry all original documents with photocopies for verification during admission.

  • Aadhar Card
  • Original Marksheet 10th/12th
  • New Passport Size Photo
  • Address Proof
  • Contact Details
